# Business-case-studies
A collection of real business-analytics case studies — each one starts with two metrics that appear to move together, and the causal story most people would jump to. The point of the repo isn't the metrics themselves; it's the diagnostic sequence used to test whether the obvious explanation actually holds, or whether it's a timing lag, a mix shift, or a confound in disguise.

Each case follows the same three-part structure:
1. **Business background** — the segment, the metrics, and the pattern observed.
2. **My analysis** — my own diagnostic approach and reasoning, written before seeing any answer.
3. **Review** — an assessment of that reasoning: what holds up, what's a blind spot, and what a more complete analysis would check.

## Why this repo

Most dashboards show *that* a metric moved. The harder and more valuable question is *why* — and the two most common ways to get that wrong are treating same-period co-movement as causation, and mistaking a population mix shift for a behavior change. Every case here is built around one of those traps.
